[h1]Reverse Overlay - Guide Lines - ATS[/h1]

Backup-camera style guide lines for reversing, and forward dots for lane position. While you are in reverse, two lines are drawn on the ground from the rear corners of your trailer (or truck, when bobtail) along the path it will actually take:

[list]
[*][b]Red[/b] for the first 3 m, [b]yellow[/b] to 8 m, [b]green[/b] out to 15 m, with ticks where the colour changes
[*]Real objects in the world, so they show in [b]every camera and in the mirrors[/b]
[*]Follows your [b]actual wheel angles[/b] (different steering locks, lift axles and multi-steer trucks)
[*]Single trailers, doubles and triples; self-steering trailer axles included
[*]Distances and line height adjustable in a settings file
[/list]

[h2]New in 0.2.0: forward dots for lane position[/h2]
In a forward gear, two square dots appear on the road ahead: [b]red[/b] where your truck's front-left corner will be and [b]green[/b] where the front-right corner will be, about 1.5 seconds from now. Keep red inside the left lane line and green inside the right one. Trailers are ignored, because they follow inside the truck's path.

[h2]Keyboard shortcut: Ctrl+Shift+F9[/h2]
Each press moves to the next mode (only while the game window has focus):
[olist]
[*][b]Forward dots only[/b]
[*][b]Reverse lines only[/b]
[*][b]Both[/b]: dots in a forward gear, lines in reverse
[*][b]Nothing[/b]: everything hidden
[/olist]
The next press starts again at forward dots only. Your choice is remembered between games; the very first start is on [b]Both[/b]. Another key can be set in the [i][hotkey][/i] section of the settings file.

[b]Updating from 0.1.0?[/b] Update the plugin DLL as well. This item now also contains the dot models the new plugin needs, and the old plugin simply ignores them.

[h2]Settings and troubleshooting[/h2]
The plugin download includes a full user guide (User_Guide.md) with installation, settings, troubleshooting and what happens after game updates.

Settings: [i]Documents\American Truck Simulator\ats_reverse_overlay.ini[/i] (created on first start, changes apply while driving).
Log: [i]Documents\American Truck Simulator\ats_reverse_overlay.log[/i]. The first lines say whether the plugin found what it needs on your game version, and which mode the shortcut is in.

[list]
[*][b]No lines or dots at all:[/b] check that the DLL is in [i]bin\win_x64\plugins[/i] and this mod is enabled, then press [b]Ctrl+Shift+F9[/b]: the mode may be set to show nothing.
[*][b]Log says "Could not create /model/rto_guides/dot_red.pmd":[/b] the mod is older than the plugin. Let Steam update this item, or update the local .scs.
[*][b]Plugin does not load at all:[/b] the DLL is not digitally signed yet; Windows Smart App Control or antivirus software may block it. See the user guide.
[*][b]After a game update:[/b] if the log says "engine functions missing", the lines switch themselves off until the plugin is updated. The game keeps running normally.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Works alongside other mods. Do not run it together with "Reverse Posture Assistant", which hooks the same part of the game. Use one or the other.
